Codee hand icon

Fusebase's corner brackets with an open palm in the middle in place of the brain. Every shape is filled with currentColor, and the viewBox is the same 6 6 36 36, so it can replace the original directly. The spark options below add a small shine to the hand; each card shows the icon large, at 24px and 16px, and on a dark chip.

Thumb options

Each thumb is drawn as one outline with the palm, so there is no dent where they meet. The large icon shows the plain hand; the row below shows it with the option D sparkle, with the option C rays, then the sparkle version at 24px, at 16px and on a dark chip.
